# Storyboard: drafting-table-film (120 BPM, 48 beats)

| Beats | Ink | What happens | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | pencil | Centre lines and pitch circles in light construction strokes | felt-piano note |
| 0.2–10 | pencil | Housing, bolt holes, ring gear, sun, three planets, each drawn on as a boiling double stroke with the pencil point visible; brand lettered at beat 2 | pencil scratch per part |
| 8.5–14.5 | pencil | Product name as hatching drawn on line by line, then its outline; housing section hatched; dimension line and Ø label; the kind lettered under the title | scratches, piano |
| 16 | → blueprint | Hard-edged flood from the pencil's last point, clearing the farthest corner in ~0.35 s | paper slap + thud |
| 17–32 | blueprint | Gear train spins up with true ratios; four callouts draw their leaders on beats 18.5, 20.5, 22.5 and 24.5 | kick, ticks, clacks, bass |
| 32 | → hologram | One horizontal scan line sweeps down | riser into a snare |
| 33–36 | hologram | The stack explodes along its axis (housing, ring, planets, sun) into an oblique view; scan-line fills by how lit each part is | hats |
| 36–40 | hologram | Specs count in, one a beat | blips |
| 40–48 | hologram | Brand, product and URL; the gears keep turning | pad chord |
