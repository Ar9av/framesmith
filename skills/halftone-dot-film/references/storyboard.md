# Storyboard: halftone-dot-film (120 BPM, 40 beats)

| Beats | Time | The dot is… | What happens | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 0–2 s | a falling mass | Drops onto a rest line and draws its own underdamped spring curve as it sweeps right | blip on each bounce peak |
| 4–8 | 2–4 s | a tittle | The curve erases, the brand rises from its baseline, the dot hops onto the first i; other tittles pop on the beat; the kind line sets | clacks, piano |
| 8–16 | 4–8 s | day one | The word sinks away, the tittle slides to the first cell; the month fills on eighth notes (kept = accent dots, missed = rings); habit, month and count | blip per kept day, tick per miss, kick |
| 16–24 | 8–12 s | a flood | The last kept day grows past the farthest corner in ~0.35 s; three features rise on the accent ground with ink bullets | thud, clacks |
| 24–29 | 12–14.5 s | the focal point | A 26 × 14 year of dots appears in a diagonal wave; dot sizes follow the data and swell around the orbiting paper dot | hats, bass |
| 29–32 | 14.5–16 s | the screen | The field zooms past the frame around the dot and the gaps close to ink | riser |
| 32–40 | 16–20 s | a tittle again | The brand rises in paper on ink; accent dots fall onto each i; offer and URL | thud, clacks, pad |
