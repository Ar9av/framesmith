obsidian-wiki just crossed 3.5k stars on GitHub

You solve a hard bug on a Tuesday. Three months later, in another repo, you solve it again, because the answer is buried in a chat log you'll never find.

obsidian-wiki gives your agent a memory. It compiles your docs, PDFs and past agent sessions into linked markdown in your own Obsidian vault, and answers with [[wikilink]] citations.

- works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and a dozen more
- no API keys and no vector database, only markdown files you own
- same model, same 38-page vault: answers to structural questions took 19s instead of 81s, and 83% were right instead of 44%
