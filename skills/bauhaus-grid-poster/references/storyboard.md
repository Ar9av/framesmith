# Storyboard: bauhaus-grid-poster (120 BPM, 0.5 s a beat)

| Beats | Time | What happens | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 0–2 s | Grid lines draw on, verticals top→down and horizontals left→right. The red circle drops 5 modules and lands on beat 2 | ticks on 8ths, a clack on landing |
| 4–15 | 2–7.5 s | Assembly, one element per beat: the yellow quarter turns 90° about the frame corner; the blue semicircle slides 3 modules; the black beam grows from the left; the red circle steps 1 module; the yellow wedge flips; the blue slab grows and splits; the dot and the red key arrive. Small caps ride the beam; the masthead sets on beat 12 | kick on quarters from beat 4, a pitched clack on every landing, bass from bar 2 |
| 16–18.5 | 8–9.3 s | Group turn: the top block rotates 90° about its centre in 12 escapement teeth of 7.5° | a tick per tooth |
| 19–22 | 9.5–11 s | The black quarter "eye" arrives and turns 180° about its centre; the vertical title wipes up the last column | |
| 24–30 | 12–15 s | Hero: a quarter disc grows in every cell and turns 90° a beat, phase-delayed along the diagonal, then retracts | a blip per diagonal, pentatonic |
| 30 | 15 s | Press lock: the drifting plates snap into register, ink +12 %, the paper jolts 2 px | riser into a thud |
| 31–36 | 15.5–18 s | Facts type on, one per beat, flush-left on column 3; then the URL | high clacks |
| 34–40 | 17–20 s | Hold: the red key keeps turning once a bar | pad chord |
