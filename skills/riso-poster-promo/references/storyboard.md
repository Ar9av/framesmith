# Storyboard: riso-poster-promo (120 BPM)

| Scene | Beats | What happens | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| slam | 0–4 | `slam` word drops glyph by glyph (1/16 apart); brand, kicker and crumbs set on beats 1–3; a blue halftone ramp rises from the foot of the page | clack per glyph, thud on frame 0 |
| echo | 4–8 | `echo` word repeats down the page five times, an eighth note apart, alternating plates with decaying ink and tape wow | blip per tap, decaying |
| product | 8–16 | Fly-through the O's counter (1 beat). Duotone halftone photo (blue shadows at 15°, pink mids at 75°); three spec numbers slam in on beats 9, 11 and 13 | riser into the cut, clacks |
| facts | 16–24 | Four inverse-video bands wipe in from alternating sides, letters scrambled until their beat | ticks then a clack per fact |
| hero | 24–32 | Product name rotated to run the full height, glyphs filled with the halftone photo over a flat pink ghost; a smash pan in from the right | thud + paper slap |
| end | 32–40 | Brand, product, kicker, price, availability and URL slam in; grain keeps breathing | clacks, pad chord |

Every impact gets about four frames of ghost-plate misregistration and a decaying punch-in.
